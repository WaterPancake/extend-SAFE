# Third-party materials

This repository reports experiments involving the following external projects:

- [SAFE](https://github.com/vla-safe/SAFE) and its
  [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09937);
- [OpenVLA](https://github.com/openvla/openvla) and its
  [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246);
- [LIBERO](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO) and its
  [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03310).

The MIT license in this repository applies only to original extend-SAFE code and
documentation. It does not replace or broaden the licenses, model terms, data
terms, or citation requirements of those projects. Raw rollouts, benchmark
assets, OpenVLA weights, and hidden-state artifacts are not distributed here.
